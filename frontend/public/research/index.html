How we research

  • Primary sources first: company filings with the SEC, official releases and exchange data.
  • No source, no number: if we can't verify a figure, we say it wasn't found.
  • Facts and interpretation are labelled separately; a coincidence in time is never presented as a cause.
  • Every calculation is re-run independently before publication.
