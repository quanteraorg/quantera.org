Fibonacci retracement

Intermediate · Technical analysis

The idea

Fibonacci retracement divides a big price move into levels where a pullback might pause, commonly 23.6%, 38.2%, 50% and 61.8%. It is a way of asking “how far back is a normal pullback?”

How it works

  • Pick a clear swing low and swing high, then the tool draws the levels between them.
  • In an uptrend the levels show possible pauses as the price falls back.
  • The 50% level is popular but isn’t really a Fibonacci ratio.

Worked example

A stock rises from $100 to $200, a $100 move. The 61.8% retracement is $200 − (0.618 × $100) = $138.20. The 38.2% level is $161.80.

Common mistakes

  • Believing the levels have predictive power by themselves. Evidence for them is mixed.
  • Choosing different swing points until the lines appear to fit.

Key takeaways

  • Levels are measured from one swing low to one swing high.
  • Use them as reference points only.
  • Be consistent about which swings you choose.
