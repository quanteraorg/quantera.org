Bond duration

Advanced · Markets

The idea

Duration measures how sensitive a bond's price is to changes in interest rates, expressed in years.

How it works

  • Price change ≈ −duration × change in yield.
  • Longer maturity and lower coupons mean higher duration.
  • Bond funds list their average duration — a quick gauge of rate risk.

Worked example

A bond fund with duration 7: if yields rise 1 percentage point, its price falls about 7%. A fund with duration 2 falls only about 2%.

Common mistakes

  • Confusing duration with maturity.
  • Holding long-duration bonds while expecting rising rates.

Key takeaways

  • Duration = rate sensitivity.
  • Price change ≈ −duration × yield change.
  • Long, low-coupon bonds are the most sensitive.
