The MACD indicator

Intermediate · Technical analysis

The idea

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) subtracts a slow average from a fast one. When the fast average pulls ahead of the slow one, recent momentum is stronger than the longer-term trend.

How it works

  • MACD line: usually the 12-period EMA minus the 26-period EMA.
  • Signal line: a 9-period EMA of the MACD line.
  • Histogram: the gap between those two lines. Growing bars mean momentum is strengthening in that direction.

Worked example

If the 12-day EMA is $105 and the 26-day EMA is $102, the MACD line is +3. If the signal line is 2, the histogram is +1, and momentum is on the upside.

Common mistakes

  • Treating every crossover as a buy or sell signal.
  • Comparing MACD values between stocks. It is measured in dollars, so a $500 stock has larger numbers than a $20 one.

Key takeaways

  • It measures momentum from two averages.
  • Watch the direction of the histogram.
  • It lags, like the averages it is built from.
