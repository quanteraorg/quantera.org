Accessibility

Last updated 13 November 2026.

We want everyone to be able to use Quantera, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, magnification or voice control. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Every change is checked automatically with axe accessibility tests on 27 pages, in light and dark themes, on desktop and phone sizes. A change that fails can’t ship.
  • Text and controls are checked for colour contrast in both themes. Price moves use ▲ and ▼ as well as colour.
  • Everything can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Form fields have labels, buttons have names, and images have text alternatives.
  • Animations stop when your device asks for reduced motion, and see-through “glass” effects turn off when it asks for reduced transparency.
  • Pages scale to fit phones, tablets and large screens without sideways scrolling.

Known limitations

  • Charts are drawn as pictures. Each has a text label, and the key figures appear as text beside it, but individual points on a chart can’t yet be read by a screen reader.
  • Some older parts of the site still use custom controls that we are replacing with standard ones.
  • We have tested with automated tools and keyboards; full testing with screen readers is still to come.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, please tell us at @quantera.research on Instagram: what page, what you were trying to do and what assistive technology you use. We’ll reply within 5 working days and fix problems as quickly as we can.
