Who we are

Quantera is a free financial research and learning website, built and run independently by its founders. It is not a bank, broker, investment adviser or research analyst. “We” and “us” in this policy mean the people who run Quantera. You can reach us at @quantera.research on Instagram.

Summary

  • You can use Quantera without an account. Most of what you do stays in your own browser.
  • We set no cookies, show no ads and never sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and error reports help us improve the site. You can turn them off in Settings, and they start off if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it yourself, in Settings.

Why we use it, and our legal bases

For visitors in the UK, EU and similar places, these are the legal bases we rely on:

  • To provide the service you ask for (contract): your account, sync, alerts and the leaderboard you join.
  • Consent: the waitlist, the leaderboard and background alerts are opt-in, and you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: anonymous statistics and error reports to improve the site, and server logs to keep it secure. You can object at any time by turning statistics and error reports off in Settings.

How long we keep it

  • Account and synced data: until you delete your account.
  • Leaderboard: until you leave it or delete your account.
  • Waitlist: until we email you that the feature has launched, then deleted; or sooner if you ask.
  • Price alerts: until you turn alerts off, delete your account, or your browser withdraws the notification address.
  • Error reports: kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.
  • Statistics: kept only as totals.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting providers for a short period, typically days.
  • Data in your browser: until you clear it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR and UK GDPR, California’s CCPA, or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you can ask to access, correct, download or delete your personal information, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

  • Delete everything: open Settings, choose Delete account and type DELETE. This removes your login, your synced data and your leaderboard entry.
  • Turn off statistics and error reports: in Settings. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are respected automatically.
  • Anything else: message us at @quantera.research on Instagram. We reply within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Anyone can read Quantera without an account. You must be at least 13 to create an account, join the leaderboard, the waitlist or alerts. If you are under 18, or under the age of digital consent where you live, please use these features only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Connections are encrypted, passwords are handled by Supabase and never seen by us, database tables are protected by row-level security, and our API keys stay on our server. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

Contact and changes

Questions or requests: @quantera.research on Instagram. If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and tell account holders about significant changes.
