Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

Quantera is a free place to learn investing with paper money. We collect as little as we can, we never sell your data and we show no ads.

What stays on your device

Your watchlists, paper portfolio, trades, notes, lesson progress and settings are saved in your browser’s storage. They never leave your device unless you create an account. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you create an account

An account is optional. It stores your email address and a copy of your progress so it follows you between devices. Sign-in is handled by our database provider, Supabase; we never see your password.

The leaderboard

If you join the leaderboard, the nickname you choose, your paper returns and your number of trades are shown publicly. Don’t use your real name if you’d rather stay anonymous. You can leave at any time.

The waitlist

If you join a waitlist, we keep your email address only to tell you when that feature launches.

Price alerts

If you turn on background alerts, your browser gives us a notification address and we store it with your alert settings so we can notify you.

Usage statistics

We count which pages and features are used, such as “viewed the leaderboard”, without attaching your name or email. We also use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies.

Services we rely on

Vercel hosts the website, Render runs our data server and Supabase stores accounts. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Market data comes from providers such as Yahoo Finance, Finnhub, Twelve Data, SEC EDGAR and FRED; your requests to them go through our server, not from your browser. If a page hits an error, a report of what went wrong (the page, the error and your browser type, but not your name, email or account details) goes to Sentry so we can fix it.

Children

Anyone can use Quantera without an account. You must be 13 or older to create an account.

Removing your data

To delete your account and everything stored with it, message us on Instagram at @quantera.research and we’ll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
