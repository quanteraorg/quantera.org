Cookies and storage

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Quantera sets no cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage, which works like a notebook kept only on your device, to remember your work and settings.

What’s stored on your device

WhatWhyNeeded?
Watchlists, paper portfolio, trades and trade journalSo your practice investing is still there next timeYes, for those features
Notes, research folders, thesis notes, recent reportsSo your research is savedYes, for those features
Lesson progress, daily-challenge streak, badgesTo track your learningYes, for those features

Sign-in session (only if you have an account)To keep you signed in; issued by SupabaseYes, for accounts
Short-lived page memory (session storage)Scroll positions and one-time notices during a visit; cleared when you close the tabYes

None of this is used for advertising or to track you across other websites.

Statistics and error reports

Vercel Web Analytics counts page views without cookies or storage on your device. Sentry error reports don’t use cookies. Both are optional: turn them off in Settings or the privacy notice, and they stay off if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Other websites

If you choose “Continue with Google”, you go to Google’s own sign-in page, where Google’s cookie policy applies. Links to news publishers and other sites take you to their websites, which have their own policies. Quantera doesn’t embed third-party ads, social media widgets or tracking pixels.

How to clear it

Clear this site’s data in your browser settings, or delete your account in Settings. Clearing browser data removes anything you haven’t synced to an account. More in our privacy policy.
